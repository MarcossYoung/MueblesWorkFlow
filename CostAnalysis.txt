  ---
  How I'd Structure It

  Recommended: One-time project fee + monthly retainer

  1. Discovery/scoping — small fixed fee ($500–$1,500) to define requirements
  2. Development — fixed price based on estimated hours × your rate
  3. Post-launch retainer — $500–$1,500/mo for hosting management, bug fixes,
  small features

  This protects you from scope creep and gives the client ongoing support.

  ---
  Key Factors That Affect Price

  - Does the client already have designs/wireframes?
  - How polished does the UI need to be?
  - Multi-tenant (multiple businesses) or single-tenant?
  - Who handles hosting costs?
  - How many revision rounds are included?
